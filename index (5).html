About me

I'm Lalith, a data analyst based in Rochester, NY, finishing my MS in Business Analytics at Simon Business School. My undergrad was in Business Administration, which shaped how I think about data: always through the lens of so what does this actually mean for the business?

I get genuinely excited about the moment a messy dataset starts making sense, whether that's writing a SQL query that finally cracks a weird pattern, or watching a model I trained go live as a real app people can use. My churn prediction project is a good example: I didn't just build a model, I deployed it as an interactive Streamlit app so anyone can actually test it.

Outside of work, I'm into stargazing, and I recently picked up an electric scooter to get around Rochester, which given the winters here, feels like a bold life choice. I'm currently looking for full-time Data Analyst or Data Scientist roles across the US where I can keep building things that matter.
